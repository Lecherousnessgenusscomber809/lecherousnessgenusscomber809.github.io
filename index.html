🔧 Troubleshooting Tips

Most problems during a download are small and easy to fix. Try these checks before anything else:

  • Nothing seems to happen: look at the top-right corner of your browser. A small download icon or arrow usually appears there while a file is being saved.
  • The page will not load: check that you are connected to the internet, then refresh the page with F5. If you are on a work network, a firewall may be blocking the site.
  • The file cannot be opened: right-click the file and choose "Properties". Windows shows a small "Unblock" checkbox at the bottom of the General tab — tick it and click OK, then try again.
  • Your antivirus blocks it: antivirus tools are cautious with newly downloaded files. Make sure your antivirus is up to date and confirm with your IT contact before allowing anything through.
  • The installer closes immediately: restart your computer once. That clears out leftover temporary files that sometimes interfere with installers.
  • You are not sure where it went: open File Explorer, click "Downloads" in the left-hand panel, and sort the list by the "Date modified" column.
